\section{Results by class and size}
\label{app:cells}

Table~\ref{tab:e-cells} gives, for each class and size, the sum of the ten instances. The columns of our
configurations are the mean of three seeds, rounded to one decimal; the totals in the last row are computed with unrounded values. \cfgfast{} and \cfgbal{} come from the repetition of November 2026 (stored result files); $\dagger$: the column of \cfgqual{} comes from an earlier campaign whose raw files were lost. The other methods are the
totals published by \citet{Goncalves2013} (Table~8 of their article; averages times ten):
BRKGA, GVND \citep{Parreno2010}, TS2P \citep{Crainic2009} and GLS \citep{Faroe2003}. LB is the sum of the lower bounds given in the header of each instance file (6852 in total;
the bound computed by our program, which is the maximum of the continuous bound and the bounds $L_1$ and $L_2$ of \citet{Martello2000}, adds up to 6837 on the same instances).
